Why publish fair lines instead of picks

Most picks sites sell a table of odds. The professional question is not “what is the line” but “which side of this price is wrong, by how much, and how confident is the model.” PropStein publishes the fair line, the acceptable price boundaries, the role evidence behind each projection, and a dated track record — including the weeks where calibration was poor.

Evidence

  • Published track record — every graded decision, including the bad ones
  • Methodology — model, gating, verification and known defects

Model limitations

PropStein publishes model limitations alongside every page. Roles and thresholds are provisional pending chronological holdout validation. In our published week-1 holdout evaluation the model's Brier score trailed the naive baseline; we treat that as an open defect, not a marketing problem, and we publish recalibration results as they land. Snapshots are taken pre-kickoff; quotes age and lines move, so every number here carries a timestamp and a verification state. Route-participation and late-scratch information are not fully modeled.
