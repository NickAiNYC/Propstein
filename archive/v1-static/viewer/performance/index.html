Market-Model Edge Fit & Guarantee Status (Phase 2 & 3)

Model has no edge over market closing lines at this time. All projections should be treated as informational.

Chronological regression fitting p = w · pmodel + (1 - w) · pmarket on active trigger decisions yields:
• ŵ = 0.6742 (Standard Error: 0.3957, 95% CI: [-0.1013, +1.4497], N = 37)
Because the 95% confidence interval spans zero, there is no statistically significant evidence of closing line alpha over the market consensus.

Public actions in effect:
• Beat-Close Guarantee Suspended: Beating or tying a close on a small sample guarantees nothing about win rate or expected value. The marketing guarantee is publicly withdrawn.
• Paid Signups Frozen & Quant Tier Waitlisted: Commercial paid upgrades remain suspended until N ≥ 300 graded board decisions achieve positive CLV bounded strictly above zero with 95% confidence.
• Uncensored Research Access: Projections and live pricing calculations are made available without paywalls in observer research mode.

What we publish as the sample grows

Calibration by predicted-probability bucket; win rate with sample size; ROI with a confidence interval; maximum drawdown; splits by sport, market and model version; and losing weeks next to winning weeks, every week. The commitment is the record, not the highlight reel.

Model limitations

PropStein publishes model limitations alongside every page. Roles and thresholds are provisional pending chronological holdout validation. In our published week-1 holdout evaluation the model's Brier score trailed the naive baseline; we treat that as an open defect, not a marketing problem, and we publish recalibration results as they land. Snapshots are taken pre-kickoff; quotes age and lines move, so every number here carries a timestamp and a verification state. Route-participation and late-scratch information are not fully modeled.
