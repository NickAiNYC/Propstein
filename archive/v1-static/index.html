MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION

Research, Calibration & SGP Bivariate Model

Full mathematical specification, out-of-sample calibration error audits, and same-game parlay joint correlation modeling.

SRC/PARLAY.PY

Bivariate Receiver Volume Simulator (Modeled Dependence)

Evaluates joint distribution of receptions and receiving yards using Marsaglia-Tsang bivariate sampling. Demonstrates how volume correlation affects joint probability relative to an unadjusted independent product. Note: Sportsbooks use proprietary correlation copulas and do NOT use naive multiplication. Compare against actual offered sportsbook SGP odds below.

Dependence Assumption Caveat: The correlation between receptions and yards varies substantially across player archetypes. Deep threats with high aDOT (e.g. Alec Pierce) have significantly lower correlation with receptions than slot possession receivers (e.g. Amon-Ra St. Brown). A uniform correlation parameter must not be assumed universally across markets.

The Negative Binomial • Compound Gamma Framework

1. COUNT MODEL (RECEPTIONS)

Receptions are modeled as a Negative Binomial random variable NegBin(μrec, α), where α is the dispersion parameter estimated from historical NFL play-by-play. This accounts for target clustering and game-script variance that Poisson models miss.

2. SEVERITY MODEL (YARDS PER REC)

Each reception produces yards following a Gamma distribution with shape parameter k and scale parameter μypr / k. Total receiving yards is the compound sum of N independent Gamma draws.

3. CALIBRATED FAIR LINES (50/50 MEDIAN)

Fair lines are derived directly from the calibrated median of the predictive distribution. A player's fair line is defined as the exact line where P(Over) = 50.0% and P(Under) = 50.0% under the calibrated model.

4. ZERO LLM IN DECISION PATH

The betting decision, expected value, and stake sizing are computed with 100% deterministic mathematical code. Generative language models are strictly restricted to synthesizing textual injury summaries and context, never bets.
