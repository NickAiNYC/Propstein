Model Evolution & Architecture Milestones

v3.0
Week 1: Poisson Baseline (Trailed Naive)

Discrete Poisson count model for receptions with constant empirical YPR. Trailed the naive baseline on chronological holdout (Brier: 0.2642 vs 0.2520). Documented and published as an open defect.

v3.1
Week 4: NegBin2 Dispersion + Target-Share EWMA

Introduced Negative Binomial variance expansion (α = 0.1097) to account for heavy right tails and target-share volatility over recent game windows.

v3.2
Week 7 holdout: Compound NegBin-Gamma Convolution

Two-stage compound continuous Gamma yardage per reception (k = 1.555). Solved continuous and discrete -110 vig boundaries with exact integer push windows.

v3.3
Roadmap: Walkforward Chronological Recalibration

Expanding prospective sample toward N = 300 descriptive checkpoint and N = 600 confirmatory gate. Adding coverage-funnel adjustments and live injury absorption tracking.

Market-Model Edge Fit & Guarantee Status (Phase 2 & 3)

Model has no edge over market closing lines at this time. All projections should be treated as informational.

Chronological regression fitting p = w · pmodel + (1 - w) · pmarket yields:
• Full-board quoted evaluation: ŵ = 0.7597 (Standard Error: 0.1835, 95% CI: [0.4001, 1.1193], N = 320 observations)
• Trigger decision cohort: ŵ = 0.6742 (Standard Error: 0.3957, 95% CI: [-0.1013, +1.4497], N = 37)
Because the decision-level 95% confidence interval spans zero and holdout Brier skill is negative (-12.51%), model projections are anchored to the devigged market prior with shrinkage toward w ≈ 0.25 to prevent overconfidence.

Public actions in effect:
• Beat-Close Guarantee Suspended: Beating or tying a close on a small sample guarantees nothing about win rate or expected value. The marketing guarantee is publicly withdrawn.
• Paid Signups Frozen & Quant Tier Waitlisted: The preregistered commercial gate requires 600 independently evaluable qualifying signals per sport, mean price CLV of at least +1.0 percentage point, and a strictly positive game-clustered lower confidence bound at one-sided α = 0.0125. N = 300 is a descriptive checkpoint, not an unlock. Compliance approval is also required. The 37 full-board decisions above are a separate published audit cohort, not the gate sample.
• Uncensored Research Access: Projections and live pricing calculations are made available without paywalls in observer research mode.

What we publish as the sample grows

Calibration by predicted-probability bucket; win rate with sample size; return with a confidence interval; maximum drawdown; splits by sport, market and model version; and losing weeks next to winning weeks, every week. The commitment is the record, not the highlight reel.

Model limitations

PropStein publishes model limitations alongside every page. Roles and thresholds are provisional pending chronological holdout validation. In our published week-1 holdout evaluation the model's Brier score trailed the naive baseline; we treat that as an open defect, not a marketing problem, and we publish recalibration results as they land. Snapshots are taken pre-kickoff; quotes age and lines move, so every number here carries a timestamp and a verification state. Route-participation and late-scratch information are not fully modeled.
