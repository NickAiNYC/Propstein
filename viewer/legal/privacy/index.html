Privacy Policy

Short version: PropStein has no accounts, asks for no personal information, and stores your UI preferences in your own browser.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no profiles. Your terminal settings (theme, layout and similar preferences) are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing site data in your browser removes them entirely.

Server and hosting logs

The site is served through a third-party host (Vercel Inc.), which processes standard request logs (such as IP address, user agent and timestamps) for security and abuse prevention under its own privacy policy. We do not use these logs to build profiles of visitors.

Analytics

We may measure aggregate traffic (page views, referrers, rough geography) using a privacy-respecting analytics provider that does not use cookies for cross-site advertising. We do not run ad-network trackers, retargeting pixels, or cross-site profiling scripts, and we do not sell or share visitor data. If we ever launch accounts or payments, this policy will be updated first.

Local storage

The terminal stores preferences under keys such as propstein-theme and UI state in local storage. These are functional, first-party, readable only by this site, and never transmitted to us. You can inspect and delete them via your browser's developer tools or site-data settings.

Children

PropStein is intended for adults of legal gambling age in their jurisdiction (21+ in most U.S. states) and is not directed to children. If you believe a minor has used the site, contact us and we will investigate.

Security, changes, contact

We use static, read-only hosting and HTTPS everywhere, which minimizes the attack surface for visitor data in the first place. We may update this policy as the product changes; material changes appear on this page with a revised date. Questions or requests: support@propstein.com.

PropStein is quantitative research, not betting advice. No wager should be placed based on any content on this site. 21+ where applicable. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
