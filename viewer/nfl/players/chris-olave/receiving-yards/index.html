How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 82.0 and on the UNDER at lines above 88.4. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (85.0), no-vig breakeven odds are +101 on the OVER (49.77% win prob) and +102 on the UNDER (49.48% win prob), with a 0.75% push.
