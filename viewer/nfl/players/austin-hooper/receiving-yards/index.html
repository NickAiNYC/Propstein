How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 14.0 and on the UNDER at lines above 16.2. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (15.0), no-vig breakeven odds are +104 on the OVER (49.10% win prob) and +105 on the UNDER (48.73% win prob), with a 2.17% push.
