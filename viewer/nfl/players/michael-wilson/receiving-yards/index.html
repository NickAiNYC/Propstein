How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 50.0 and on the UNDER at lines above 54.9. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (52.5), no-vig breakeven odds are +100 on the OVER (49.92% win prob) and −100 on the UNDER (50.08% win prob).
