How to use the fair line

At −110, on non-push lines the model clears breakeven on the OVER at lines below 1.0 and on the UNDER at lines above 1.0. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the −110 breakeven on non-push lines. A whole-number line can push; its expected return must be checked using P(win) × 100/110 − P(lose). At the fair line (1.5), push-adjusted fair odds are +176 on the OVER (36.24% win prob) and −176 on the UNDER (63.76% win prob).
