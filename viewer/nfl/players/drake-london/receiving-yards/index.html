How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 51.8 and on the UNDER at lines above 56.7. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (54.0), no-vig breakeven odds are +101 on the OVER (49.74% win prob) and +103 on the UNDER (49.29% win prob), with a 0.97% push.
