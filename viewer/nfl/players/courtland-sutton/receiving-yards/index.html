How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 34.3 and on the UNDER at lines above 38.1. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (36.0), no-vig breakeven odds are +102 on the OVER (49.61% win prob) and +104 on the UNDER (49.14% win prob), with a 1.25% push.
