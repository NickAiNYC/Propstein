How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 8.0 and on the UNDER at lines above 9.0. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (8.5), no-vig breakeven odds are +107 on the OVER (48.42% win prob) and −107 on the UNDER (51.58% win prob).
