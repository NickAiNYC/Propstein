How to use the fair line

At −110 vig, the model sees value on the OVER at lines below 76.9 and on the UNDER at lines above 82.8. Between those boundaries, neither side clears the vig-adjusted breakeven. At the fair line (80.0), no-vig breakeven odds are +102 on the OVER (49.49% win prob) and +101 on the UNDER (49.70% win prob), with a 0.81% push.
