How PropStein Works: Model, Gating and Verification

PropStein is a decision terminal for player-prop markets. Instead of selling picks, it publishes what a professional needs to trust a price: the model's fair line, the price boundaries where value exists, the evidence behind each projection, and a dated, falsifiable track record.

The model

Projections use a Negative-Binomial / Gamma mixture (v3.2) over receiving volume: per-player target-share roles (recent window vs. an EWMA baseline), per-target yardage distributions, and a compounding receptions×yards CDF for yardage markets. Each snapshot stores its distribution parameters, so every published number is reproducible from its inputs.

Distribution parameterisation & compound process

Player receiving volume is modeled as a two-stage compounded process: a discrete event distribution for receptions compounded by a continuous gamma distribution for yards per catch. Every published player-prop projection is strictly reproducible from four saved snapshot parameters:

SymbolNameRole in ModelDistribution Definition
mu_recMean Expected ReceptionsCenter of reception countN ~ NegBin(μrec, α)
alphaReception DispersionVariance expansion over PoissonVar(N) = μrec + αμrec2
mu_yprMean Yards Per ReceptionExpected yardage gain per catchXi ~ Gamma(k, θ = μypr / k)
kYards Gamma ShapeSkewness and tail weight per playVar(Xi) = μypr2 / k

The total receiving yardage random variable Y = ∑i=1N Xi has an exact unconditional expectation:

E[Y] = μrec × μypr

For any yardage line L, the exceedance probability P(Y > L) is computed by convoluting the discrete Negative Binomial distribution with the cumulative Gamma distribution for N catches:

P(Y > L) = ∑n=1∞ P(N = n) · [1 − FGamma(L; shape = n · k, scale = μypr / k)]

On a non-integer line that survival is P(over) and the push is zero, so P(under) = 1 − P(over). On an integer line L the published probabilities use a half-yard push window: P(push) is the mass in (L − 0.5, L + 0.5], P(over) is the survival above L + 0.5, and P(under) is the CDF at L − 0.5. Receptions are discrete: a whole-number line pushes on that exact count and a half-point line does not. The three probabilities are normalized to sum to 1 before display, and the percentages on each page are rounded so they still sum to 100%.

Worked reproduction: A.J. Brown (Week 7 Snapshot)

Parameters: mu_rec = 5.2319, mu_ypr = 12.9847, alpha = 0.1097, k = 1.555. The same solver that builds the player pages (src/value_boundaries.py) produces the figures below. At −110 the breakeven win probability is 110/210 ≈ 52.38%.

  • Projected Volume: 5.2319 × 12.9847 = 67.93 yards.
  • Fair Line (Median): 61.0 yards.
  • Probabilities at 61.0 (integer push window): P(over) = 49.33% (+103 fair); P(under) = 49.75% (+101 fair); P(push) = 0.92%. The three displayed percentages sum to 100%.
  • Vig-adjusted −110 boundaries: P(over) equals the 52.38% breakeven at 58.23 yards and P(under) equals it at 63.37 yards. Player pages round those roots to one decimal: value on the OVER below 58.2 and on the UNDER above 63.4. Those roots are this player's distribution, not a fixed anchor. A flat push subtracted from every line would move the under root about a yard higher; the model only assigns that push on a whole-number line.

Snapshots, not vibes

Every number on this site comes from a pre-kickoff snapshot with an explicit timestamp, a content hash and a verification state (verified / unverified / stale / blocked). Quotes are captured with source and observation time. Live pricing in the terminal is never served from cache: an unavailable quote is considered safer than a stale one presented as current.

Execution gating

Rows are gated by an explicit qualification policy (edge hurdle and Kelly cap) before they can be actioned. Circuit breakers per market stop automated dispatch when feeds degrade. Availability changes (outages, usage shifts) propagate to impacted teammates as quantified volume deltas, not hunches.

Known defects, published

On our week-1 chronological holdout the model's Brier score was 0.2813 against a naive baseline of 0.25 (skill -12.51%) — i.e. the model currently trails the baseline, and we say so everywhere. Role labels remain provisional. Route-participation data is incomplete for part of the roster. Recalibration work is ongoing; until the skill score turns positive, treat all published probabilities as research, not signal.

FAQ

Is this betting advice?
No. PropStein is quantitative research about player-prop prices. Nothing here recommends a wager, and the track-record page exists so you can audit the model yourself.
Why show losing calibration publicly?
Because a record you cannot audit is marketing. Publishing the full-board calibration, including weeks where only 2 of 37 decisions beat the close, is what makes the good weeks worth believing.
How current are the numbers?
Every page carries its snapshot timestamp and quote status. Lines move constantly; the terminal re-verifies prices before any execution decision and blocks action when feeds are stale.

Model limitations

PropStein publishes model limitations alongside every page. Roles and thresholds are provisional pending chronological holdout validation. In our published week-1 holdout evaluation the model's Brier score trailed the naive baseline; we treat that as an open defect, not a marketing problem, and we publish recalibration results as they land. Snapshots are taken pre-kickoff; quotes age and lines move, so every number here carries a timestamp and a verification state. Route-participation and late-scratch information are not fully modeled.
